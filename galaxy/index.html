Stress test

Review cost grows with galaxies

Seven or eight groups per lesson pushes the diff view toward the 4x review cost it exists to avoid.

Fix: cap active groups per lesson at 3 or 4 and rotate the rest.

Setup time can erase the savings

Survey entry, curation, and dragging all happen before a lesson exists. The north star is net minutes saved per lesson taught.

Fix: time this flow with a real teacher. The timer above uses a 5 minute target.

A change regenerates every recipe

A monthly interest change touches every active recipe. Eager regeneration spends compute on lessons nobody opens.

Fix: default to lazy regeneration on next open. Try both in the recipes card.

Open questions

What does the red ring mean?

The transcript says a red circle appears when a kid lands in a group they are "actually interested in". Read as a mismatch warning here. Confirm the polarity.

How do survey results get in?

The call says kids submit five loves. It does not say whether that is paper, a form, or a paste. Paste is the only option that keeps V1 free of a student surface.
